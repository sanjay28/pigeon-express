Pigeon Express

Support for the iPhone game. Carry more mail, risk it all.

Get help

Found a bug, have a question about your purchase, or want to suggest something? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed). Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

How to play

Tap anywhere to flap. Fly through letters to collect them. Every letter makes your pigeon heavier, so flaps get weaker and the wind pushes harder. Swoop low over a mailbox to post everything you carry and bank your score, or fly over it to keep collecting for a bigger bundle. A hazard hit spills half your satchel, and a hit with an empty satchel ends the delivery. After a failed delivery you can resume from the last mailbox you posted at.

Full game purchase

Routes 1 and 2 are free. A single one-time in-app purchase unlocks routes 3 to 15. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

Restore a purchase: open the game, tap Unlock 3–15 (or any locked route), then Restore Purchases. Restore is also in Settings. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Refunds are handled by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress and settings

Your progress, stars and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. The purchase itself is tied to your Apple Account and can always be restored.

Music, sound effects, haptics and Reduce Motion can be changed in the game's Settings. Reduce Motion follows your iPhone setting by default.
