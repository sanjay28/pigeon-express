Privacy Policy

Pigeon Express for iPhone · Last updated 24 October 2026

In short: Pigeon Express does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data the app keeps on your device

The game saves your progress (stars, best scores, contracts, postmarks, Daily Dispatch results, Flight School results, best-run replays), your chosen courier and style, and your settings. This is stored only on your device using standard iOS storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

The one-time Full Game purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing a postcard

If you choose to share a postcard of a delivery, the image is created on your device and passed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is your choice. The image contains game information only (route, score, letters posted, stars).

Network, tracking and third parties

The game plays fully offline. It contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised through the support page.
